About SlowFolio

Understand history, and understand ourselves.

Who we are

SlowFolio is a digital humanities knowledge publication. We take history as the entry point, knowledge as the foundation, and human nature as the core, writing long reads worth slowing down for — so readers can understand the world, and themselves.

What we write

Six themes: History & Civilization, Money & Human Nature, Science & the World, People & Society, Books & Ideas, and Taiwan. Every article is a complete long read with a prologue, chapters and an epilogue, published with a full English version.

How we write

Core facts in every article — dates, figures, names, quotations — are verified before publication. What cannot be verified is not written; nothing is invented. Historical fact, scholarly inference and authorial opinion are kept apart, and questions without a reliable answer are stated as such.

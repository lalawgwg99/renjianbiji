Corrections Policy

When we get something wrong, how we fix it.

Principle

Once a factual error is confirmed, it is corrected — not quietly passed over. The article is updated and the correction is noted at the end.

Reporting

If you spot a factual error, please tell us through our contact details (to be announced), with the correct source, and we will check and act on it.

Scope

This policy covers factual errors. Differences of opinion or interpretation are matters for discussion, not correction.

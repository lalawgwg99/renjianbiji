Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we do not.

What we do not collect

This site has no membership system, requires no registration, and collects no names, email addresses or postal addresses.

Browser local storage

Bookmarks, font size, reading tone and resume-reading progress are stored only in your own browser’s localStorage. They are never sent to a server, and clearing browser data removes them.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is not connected. No traffic analytics service is currently running on this site; if one is enabled in future, this page will be updated.

Newsletter

Newsletter subscription is not connected, and no email addresses are collected.

External links

Source links in articles point to third-party websites, whose privacy practices are their own.
